ABOUT

A studio built around one person, on purpose.

Wallflow Studio is run by one designer and developer — Sparsh — rather than passed between a rotating team. That's a deliberate choice, not a limitation.

Wallflow

FOUNDER — DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

Hi, I'm Sparsh.

I started Wallflow Studio because most small businesses I saw had three different problems being handled by three different people — a website that looked outdated, a social page posted whenever someone remembered, and ads that never got looked at again after launch.

Wallflow puts all three under one roof, with one person who actually knows your brand end to end. I work closely with small businesses, local shops, and startups across India and beyond — handling the design, the build, and the day-to-day online presence, so you can focus on running the business.

How I work

One point of contact

You talk to the person actually doing the work, not an account manager relaying messages.

Built to be extended

Every website is set up so social and ads can plug straight into it later — not rebuilt from scratch.

No unnecessary complexity

If a simpler page or a smaller ad budget gets you the same result, that's what you'll get.

Let's talk about your business

A short call is usually enough to figure out what would actually help — no obligation to start anything.
